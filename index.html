The hike

We start in the East at Bukhansan Ui Station. We tackle the steepest and most direct climb first while our legs are fresh, ascending stone steps and mountain paths towards Baegundae Peak (836m), Seoul's highest point.

The final ridge to the peak involves smooth granite rock faces secured with heavy steel cables. After the 360° panoramic summit view, we make a gradual, scenic descent down the lush western valley toward the Bukhansanseong entrance.

Optional · Heungguksa Temple

~25 minutes extra round trip.

  • Option A: Visit the historic Heungguksa Temple at the foot of Mount Nogosan, with a view back toward the Bukhansan peaks.
  • Option B: Skip the temple, go straight to local food spots for traditional Pajeon & Makgeolli, then take the bus to Gupabal Station.

⚠️ Clothing & Footwear

Something to cover your shoulders: Bring a light layer such as a windbreaker, overshirt, or scarf if visiting Heungguksa Temple.

This is useful for covering your shoulders on the temple grounds. If entering a prayer hall, remove your shoes and keep your socks on.

Sturdy footwear: Shoes with good grip are recommended, especially for the smooth granite and rocky sections near the summit.
